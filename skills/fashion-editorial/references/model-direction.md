# Model Direction Vocabulary for Nano Banana 2

## Pose Types

### Standing Poses
- **Contrapposto**: Weight on one leg, opposite hip drops, classic S-curve. Elegant, timeless.
- **Power stance**: Feet shoulder-width apart, squared to camera. Confident, editorial.
- **Lean**: Against wall/pillar/surface, one shoulder or back. Casual, cool.
- **Weight shift**: One foot forward, slight bend in knee. Natural, relaxed.
- **Cross-legged**: One ankle crossed in front. Casual, feminine.

### Walking/Movement
- **Mid-stride**: One foot forward, natural arm swing. Dynamic, lifestyle.
- **Approaching camera**: Walking toward lens. Confident, campaign hero.
- **Walking away, looking back**: Over-shoulder glance. Mysterious, fashion.
- **Wind-in-motion**: Hair and fabric caught in breeze. Dynamic, aspirational.

### Sitting
- **Cross-legged floor**: Casual, lifestyle, relatable.
- **Perched on edge**: Sitting on table/ledge edge, one foot dangling. Cool, editorial.
- **Reclined**: Leaning back on hands or surface. Relaxed, intimate.
- **Knees pulled up**: On chair/couch with knees drawn. Cozy, lifestyle.

### Close-Up/Beauty
- **Chin tilt**: Slight tilt up (confident) or down (coy/intimate).
- **3/4 profile**: Face turned ~45° from camera. Showing cheekbone structure.
- **Hand near face**: Touching chin, cheek, or hair. Beauty editorial standard.
- **Eyes closed**: Showing eye makeup, peaceful, dreamy.
- **Looking up/down**: Through lashes (seductive) or contemplative.

## Hand Positions
- **In pockets**: One or both hands. Casual, confident, streetwear.
- **Adjusting clothing**: Collar, cuff, hem. Natural, shows garment detail.
- **Touching hair**: Behind ear, running through, holding back. Beauty, feminine.
- **Crossed arms**: Confident, editorial, power.
- **On hip**: One hand. Confident, fashion standard.
- **Holding product**: Natural grip, not death-grip. Product lifestyle.
- **Gesturing**: Mid-conversation hand movement. Candid, storytelling.
- **Near face but not touching**: Hovering near jaw/temple. Beauty editorial.
- **Clasped behind back**: Elegant, formal, structured.

## Gaze Direction
- **Direct to camera**: Confrontational, confident, connection with viewer.
- **Camera-left / camera-right**: Thoughtful, storytelling, editorial.
- **Downward**: Contemplative, modest, showing eye makeup.
- **Over shoulder at camera**: Mysterious, flirtatious, dynamic.
- **Up and away**: Aspirational, dreamy, hopeful.
- **At product/object**: Draws viewer attention to what they're looking at.
- **Eyes closed**: Peaceful, sensual, self-absorbed moment.

## Expression Library
- **Neutral composed**: Fashion standard. Mouth closed, relaxed jaw.
- **Slight smile, closed mouth**: Approachable, lifestyle, beauty.
- **Open laugh**: Genuine joy. Lifestyle, brand campaign.
- **Smirk / half-smile**: Confident, knowing, editorial edge.
- **Intense / piercing**: Strong eye contact, defined jaw. High fashion.
- **Soft / vulnerable**: Slightly parted lips, gentle eyes. Intimate, beauty.
- **Surprised / playful**: Open mouth, wide eyes. Fun, young brand.
- **Serene**: Relaxed everything, almost meditative. Wellness, skincare.

## Body Language by Brand Mood

| Brand Mood | Pose | Hands | Gaze | Expression |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Luxury | Contrapposto or power stance | Near face or crossed | Direct | Neutral composed or intense |
| Casual Lifestyle | Walking or weight shift | In pockets or natural | Slightly off-camera | Warm smile or laugh |
| Streetwear | Lean or power stance | In pockets or crossed arms | Direct or confrontational | Neutral or smirk |
| Romantic/Feminine | Soft S-curve or sitting | Touching hair or near face | Down or camera-right | Soft, slight smile |
| Sporty/Active | Mid-movement or dynamic | Active, gesturing | Forward/at camera | Determined or joyful |
| Minimalist | Still, geometric | Deliberate, symmetrical | Direct | Neutral |
| Bohemian | Flowing, relaxed | Natural, loose | Away or closed eyes | Serene or free-spirited |

## Outfit-Driven Pose Adjustments

- **Oversized coat**: Hands in pockets, coat slightly open, walking for movement
- **Fitted dress**: Contrapposto, one hand on hip, showing silhouette
- **Wide-leg pants**: Walking shot to show drape and movement
- **Accessories focus**: Hand near face for jewelry, wrist turn for watch, stepping for shoes
- **Layered outfit**: Adjusting outer layer (pulling collar, opening jacket) to show layers
- **Knitwear/Cozy**: Hugging self, pulled sleeves, touching collar — texture emphasis
- **Formal/Suiting**: Structured pose, buttoning jacket, adjusting cuff — precision gestures
