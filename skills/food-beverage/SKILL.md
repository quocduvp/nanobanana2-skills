# Food & Beverage Prompt Specialist

Generate Nano Banana 2 prompts for professional food and beverage photography — hero dishes, flat lays, restaurant ambiance, and brand content.

## Food Photography Categories

### 1. Hero Dish / Single Item

Template:
```
Appetizing [food photography style: editorial / rustic / minimal / dark moody] photograph of
[dish name with full visual description: ingredients visible, texture, color, garnish, plating
style, plate/bowl description]. [Sauce drizzle / steam rising / cheese pull / crumb scatter —
action element that adds life]. Placed on [surface: dark slate / rustic wood / white marble /
ceramic tile / linen cloth]. [Props: complementary ingredients scattered nearby / cutlery /
napkin / herbs / spice bowls — keep minimal]. [Lighting: soft natural side-light from window
on left / dramatic backlight creating rim glow / overhead diffused]. Shot on [camera] with
[90mm macro or 85mm], [f/2.8]. Shallow depth of field with sharpest focus on [hero element
of the dish]. [Color: warm and rich / bright and fresh / dark and moody]. Professional food
photography, [mood: comforting / luxurious / fresh / indulgent]. No artificial-looking food,
no plastic shine, no unnatural colors, no distracting background clutter.
```

## Food Styling Action Elements

These "hero moments" make food photos come alive — always include one:

- **Steam/heat**: "visible steam rising from the surface"
- **Pour/drizzle**: "honey being drizzled from above" / "sauce being poured"
- **Pull/stretch**: "melted cheese stretching between slices" / "chopsticks lifting noodles"
- **Break/cut**: "bread being torn apart revealing soft interior" / "knife cutting through crispy crust"
- **Scatter**: "sesame seeds mid-fall" / "powdered sugar dusting"
- **Condensation**: "cold droplets on glass surface"
- **Hands**: "hands breaking apart a croissant" / "hand reaching for a slice"

## Color Palette by Cuisine

- **Vietnamese**: Golden (broth), vibrant green (herbs), red-orange (chili), warm wood
- **Japanese**: Clean white, natural wood, deep black (lacquer), subtle green
- **Italian**: Warm terracotta, olive green, rich red (tomato), golden (olive oil)
- **French pastry**: Soft pink, cream, gold, powdered white
- **BBQ/Grill**: Dark char, amber, smoky warmth, rich browns
- **Healthy/Vegan**: Bright greens, purple (acai, berries), white (bowls, yogurt), natural
