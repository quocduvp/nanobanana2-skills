# Skincare & Beauty Prompt Specialist

Generate Nano Banana 2 prompts for skincare, cosmetics, beauty editorial, and wellness imagery.

## Skincare/Beauty Photography Categories

### 1. Product Hero — Skincare

Template:
```
Luxurious skincare product photograph of [product: bottle/tube/jar shape, material (frosted
glass, matte plastic, metallic cap), color, size, texture of product if visible (creamy,
gel, liquid, oil sheen)]. [Product interaction: slight product smear beside it / dropper with
serum drop mid-fall / cream texture swatch on surface / product reflected in surface].
Placed on [premium surface: polished white marble with grey veining / frosted glass / wet
stone / water surface with ripples / rose petals scattered]. [Lighting: soft ethereal glow
from behind creating halo effect / clean beauty lighting from front-left with subtle shadow /
backlight through translucent product showing color]. Shot on [camera] with [90-100mm macro],
[f/2.8-4]. [Color: clean and bright with soft pastels / warm golden luxury / cool clinical
white]. Shallow depth of field. Premium skincare brand photography, [mood: luxurious / pure /
scientific / natural]. No fingerprints on product, no dust, no harsh reflections obscuring
label area, no unrealistic glow.
```

Texture swatches to pair with product type:
- **Serum**: Golden or clear droplets, viscous drip
- **Cream/Moisturizer**: Soft swirl smear, thick texture dollop
- **Oil**: Glossy puddle with light catching through it
- **Toner/Mist**: Fine mist spray, water droplets
- **Mask**: Thick spread, green/clay texture
- **Lip product**: Swipe swatch on surface or on skin

## Skincare Color Palettes by Brand Archetype

- **Clinical/Scientific**: White, silver, ice blue, minimal — think medical-grade
- **Luxury/Premium**: Gold, marble, deep emerald, black, velvet textures
- **Clean/Natural**: Sage green, cream, terracotta, raw linen, botanical elements
- **K-Beauty**: Soft pink, lavender, baby blue, cute minimal, glass-like surfaces
- **Indie/Artisan**: Earth tones, handmade ceramics, dried flowers, kraft paper

## Beauty Lighting Setups

- **Butterfly/Paramount**: Light directly above and in front — classic beauty, creates small shadow under nose
- **Clamshell**: Key light above + fill below (or reflector) — eliminates under-eye shadows, pure beauty
- **Ring light**: Even, shadowless, creates distinctive circular catchlights — social media beauty
- **Backlit ethereal**: Light behind subject creating rim/halo — dreamy, skincare hero moments
- **Window light + reflector**: Natural, soft, editorial — lifestyle skincare content
