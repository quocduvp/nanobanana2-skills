| Focal Length | Use Case | Character |
|---|---|---|
| 24mm | Environmental, architecture, wide establishing | Dramatic perspective, slight distortion |
| 28mm | Street, documentary, travel | Wide but natural, immersive |
| 35mm | Lifestyle, environmental portrait, food flat lay | Natural field of view, versatile |
| 50mm | Standard portrait, product, lifestyle | Closest to human eye, neutral |

## Aperture Guide

| Aperture | Effect | Best For |
|---|---|---|
| f/1.2 - f/1.4 | Extremely shallow DOF, dreamy bokeh | Artistic portraits, mood shots |
| f/1.8 - f/2 | Shallow DOF, subject isolation | Lifestyle, beauty, fashion |
| f/2.8 | Balanced sharpness + bokeh | Product, fashion, food |

| f/5.6 - f/8 | Deep sharp, environmental visible | E-commerce full outfit, food spread |
| f/11+ | Everything sharp | Architecture, wide landscape |

## Film Stocks

### Warm / Portrait
- **Kodak Portra 400**: Industry standard — warm skin, soft contrast, slightly lifted shadows. THE fashion/beauty/lifestyle default.
- **Kodak Portra 160**: More subtle than 400, finer grain, cleaner skin. Beauty/skincare preferred.
- **Kodak Portra 800**: Grainy warmth, great for low light lifestyle, evening events.

### Vivid / Saturated
- **Fujifilm Velvia 50**: Extreme color saturation, punchy. Food, landscape, vivid product.
- **Kodak Ektar 100**: Vivid but more natural than Velvia. Street, travel, dynamic lifestyle.
- **Kodak Gold 200**: Consumer warmth, nostalgic, casual lifestyle, summer vibes.

### Soft / Airy
- **Fujifilm Pro 400H**: Soft pastel, airy highlights, slightly cool. Ethereal lifestyle, wedding-like.
- **Fujifilm Superia 400**: Consumer film, slightly green/cool, casual retro.

### Black & White
- **Kodak Tri-X 400**: Classic grainy B&W, punchy contrast. Editorial, street, dramatic.
- **Ilford HP5 Plus**: Softer B&W, finer grain. Portrait, artistic.
- **Kodak T-Max 100**: Ultra sharp B&W, minimal grain. Beauty, product.

### Cinematic / Night
- **CineStill 800T**: Tungsten-balanced, warm highlights, cyan shadows, halation glow around lights. Night scenes, restaurant, bar, cinematic mood.
- **CineStill 50D**: Daylight cinema film, rich colors, cinematic but daytime.

## Lighting Setups

### Portrait/Beauty
- **Butterfly/Paramount**: Key light directly above and slightly in front. Small shadow under nose. Classic beauty.
- **Rembrandt**: Key light 45° to side and above. Triangle of light on shadow-side cheek. Dramatic portrait.
- **Loop**: Key light 30-45° to side. Small shadow from nose loops toward cheek. Most versatile.
- **Split**: Light from direct side, half face lit, half in shadow. Dramatic, editorial.
- **Clamshell**: Key above + fill below. Eliminates under-eye shadows. Pure beauty lighting.
- **Rim/Edge**: Light from behind creating bright outline. Separation, dramatic, hair light.

### Product
- **Soft key + fill**: Main soft light from one side, fill card on opposite. Clean product.
- **Backlight through product**: Light behind translucent products (bottles, liquids). Premium feel.
- **Top-down even**: Flat overhead diffusion. Flat lay standard.
- **Dramatic single source**: One directional light, strong shadows. Luxury/moody product.

### Natural/Lifestyle
- **Golden hour**: Warm directional sunlight 0-20° above horizon. The lifestyle standard.
- **Blue hour**: Cool ambient after sunset. Moody, cinematic.
- **Overcast**: Even, soft, shadowless. Clean lifestyle, fashion lookbook.
- **Window light**: Soft directional from one side. Indoor lifestyle, food, beauty.
- **Dappled light**: Filtered through trees/blinds. Organic, romantic.
- **Backlit/Contre-jour**: Subject between camera and light source. Ethereal, dreamy.

## Composition Terms

- **Rule of thirds**: Subject placed on intersection of third-lines
- **Centered/Symmetrical**: Subject dead center, often with symmetrical background
- **Golden ratio/spiral**: Natural flowing composition following phi spiral
- **Leading lines**: Environmental lines drawing eye to subject
- **Framing**: Using environmental elements (doorway, window, branches) to frame subject
- **Negative space**: Intentional empty area — specify location and percentage for text overlay
- **Fill the frame**: Subject takes up most of the image, minimal background
- **Diagonal composition**: Key elements arranged along diagonal for dynamic energy

## Camera Angles

- **Eye-level**: Neutral, relatable, standard
- **Slightly low (15-30°)**: Empowering, fashion, commanding
- **Low angle (45°+)**: Dramatic, powerful, editorial
- **Slightly high (15-30°)**: Intimate, approachable, beauty
- **High angle (45°+)**: Vulnerable, small, overhead context
- **Top-down/Bird's eye**: Flat lay, food spread, outfit layout
- **Dutch angle**: Tilted frame, dynamic, editorial edge
