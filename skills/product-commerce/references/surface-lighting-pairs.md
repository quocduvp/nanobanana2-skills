## General Rules

1. **Match surface premium to product premium**: Luxury product → marble/velvet/glass. Casual product → wood/linen/concrete.
2. **Contrast, don't match**: Light product on dark surface, dark product on light surface.
3. **Reflective surfaces add perceived value** but require careful lighting to avoid unwanted reflections.
4. **Matte surfaces are safer** for beginners — more forgiving with lighting.
5. **Wet surfaces** (misted marble, rain-splashed concrete) add dimension and freshness.
6. **Texture contrast**: Smooth product on rough surface, or vice versa.
