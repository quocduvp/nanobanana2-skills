# TikTok Ad Frameworks & Storyboard Templates

## Framework 1: HOOK → PROBLEM → SOLUTION → PROOF → CTA

The classic direct-response format. High conversion for product-focused ads.

### Clip-by-Clip Breakdown

| Clip | Duration | Content | Nano Banana 2 Frame | Motion Style |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Hook | 1-2 sec | Scroll-stopping visual or statement | Close-up of surprising result / person reacting | Quick push-in (Kling) or voice hook (Veo 3) |
| 2. Problem | 2-3 sec | Relatable pain point | Person frustrated / "before" state shown | Handheld UGC feel, head shake |
| 3. Solution | 2-3 sec | Product introduction | Product reveal, unboxing, first look | Slow dolly-in, dramatic reveal |
| 4. Proof | 3-5 sec | Demo, texture, result, before/after | Product in use, close-up detail | Static macro + slow orbit |
| 5. CTA | 1-2 sec | Product hero + call to action | Clean product shot with text space | Static or subtle float + text overlay |

**Total: 9-15 seconds**

## Framework 2: STORY → PRODUCT PLACEMENT (Lifestyle Integration)

Soft-sell approach. The product fits naturally into an aspirational scene.

| Clip | Duration | Content | Frame Concept |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Scene set | 2-3 sec | Aspirational morning / evening / weekend | Wide lifestyle shot, warm lighting |
| 2. Activity | 3-5 sec | Person in relatable routine | Medium shot, natural movement |
| 3. Product moment | 2-3 sec | Organic product interaction | Close-up hands + product |
| 4. Enjoyment | 2-3 sec | Satisfaction, smile, confidence | Medium portrait, warm mood |
| 5. Product + brand | 1-2 sec | Clean product shot or logo | Product hero shot |

**Total: 10-16 seconds**

---

## Framework 3: TRANSFORMATION (Before → After)

Powerful for skincare, fashion, home, fitness.

| Clip | Duration | Content | Frame Concept |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Before (hook) | 1-2 sec | "Before" state — relatable, not unflattering | Candid, natural lighting |
| 2. Process | 3-5 sec | Using the product / making the change | Action shots, close-ups |
| 3. Transition | 1-2 sec | Dramatic shift moment | Whip pan / fade / first-to-last frame |
| 4. After (reveal) | 2-3 sec | Stunning "after" result | Polished, beautiful lighting |
| 5. Product credit | 1-2 sec | Product hero | Clean, premium |

**Total: 8-14 seconds**

---

## Framework 4: UGC REVIEW / "HONEST OPINION"

Trust-building format. Highest engagement rate.

| Clip | Duration | Content | Frame Concept |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Direct address | 1-2 sec | "Okay so I need to talk about this" | Medium close-up, authentic setting |
| 2. Product show | 2-3 sec | Holding up product, showing packaging | Hands + product, natural light |
| 3. Demo | 3-5 sec | Actually using the product | Close-up application / use |
| 4. Reaction | 2-3 sec | Genuine reaction to result | Face close-up, authentic expression |
| 5. Verdict | 1-2 sec | "10/10 would buy again" type statement | Same angle as clip 1, smile |

**Total: 9-15 seconds**

---

## Framework 5: ASMR / SENSORY

Huge on TikTok. Works for food, skincare, unboxing, stationery.

| Clip | Duration | Content | Audio (Veo 3) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Close-up | 2-3 sec | Extreme close-up of texture/surface | Quiet room tone |
| 2. Touch/interact | 2-3 sec | Fingers touching texture, opening | Close-mic contact sounds |
| 3. Main action | 3-5 sec | Core ASMR moment (pour, spread, unwrap) | Satisfying primary sound |
| 4. Result | 2-3 sec | Final state — beautiful arrangement | Soft ambient, gentle sigh |

**Total: 9-14 seconds (best with Veo 3 for audio)**

## Framework 6: TREND / TRANSITION FORMAT

Leverages TikTok trends — outfit changes, makeovers, room reveals.

| Clip | Duration | Content | Technique |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Start state | 1-2 sec | Casual / before | Static, authentic |
| 2. Trigger | 0.5-1 sec | Hand clap / snap / spin | Veo 3 first+last frame transition |
| 3. End state | 2-3 sec | Transformed / styled / revealed | Dramatic reveal, slow motion |

**Total: 3-6 seconds per transition (can chain multiple)**

## Hook Library — First Frame Ideas

### Visual Hooks
- Extreme macro of an unexpected texture (cream swirl, fabric weave, food cross-section)
- Hands about to open/reveal something (anticipation)
- Split screen: ugly vs beautiful (before/after tease)
- POV looking down at something surprising
- Product suspended mid-air / mid-pour / mid-fall

### Text Hook Templates (overlay on first frame)
- "I found the [product category] that actually works"
- "POV: you finally discover [benefit]"
- "This [product] is going viral for a reason"
- "Stop scrolling if you [relatable problem]"
- "The [product] that [unexpected benefit]"
- "I've tested 50+ [products] and this one wins"
- "Why didn't anyone tell me about this sooner"

### Audio Hooks (Veo 3)
- Trending sound snippet (reference the trend in prompt)
- Dramatic voice: "Okay but watch this..."
- ASMR trigger sound: satisfying click/snap/pour
- Genuine surprise reaction: "Wait... WHAT?"

## Platform-Specific Adjustments

### TikTok
- Hook in first 0.5 seconds — most aggressive
- Trending audio is king — reference in Veo 3 prompt
- Text overlays: big, readable, in safe zone
- Authentic > Polished (UGC aesthetic wins)
- Loop-friendly: end state visually connects to start

### Instagram Reels
- Slightly more polished than TikTok
- Caption text can be longer, more descriptive
- Carousel-first platform: consider still images + one video
- Stories: add interactive elements (poll, question sticker)

### YouTube Shorts
- Can be longer (up to 60 sec) and more informational
- Thumbnail matters — first frame IS the thumbnail
- Less music-dependent, more voice/information driven
- "How to" and educational angles work well
