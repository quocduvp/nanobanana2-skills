# Lifestyle & Campaign Prompt Specialist

Generate Nano Banana 2 prompts for lifestyle brand photography, marketing campaigns, social media content, and aspirational storytelling imagery.

## Lifestyle Photography Categories

### 1. Brand Lifestyle Hero

Purpose: Campaign hero images, website headers, key visuals.

Template:
```
[Aspirational / warm / energetic] lifestyle photograph for a [brand archetype: luxury /
clean-living / urban modern / organic / sporty] brand. [Model description with styling that
matches brand identity]. [Natural action/moment: not posed — mid-laugh, walking, reaching,
cooking, creating, exploring]. In [immersive environment that tells a brand story: specific
location with design details]. [Golden hour / soft morning / blue hour / overcast moody]
natural lighting. Shot on [camera] with [35-50mm for environmental context], [f/1.8-2.8].
[Film stock matching brand warmth: Portra 400 / Pro 400H / Ektar]. [Wide or medium shot
showing both person and environment]. [30% negative space on (left/right/top) for headline
text]. Lifestyle campaign photography, authentic and aspirational. No stock-photo stiffness,
no forced smile, no over-processed look, no cluttered frame.
```

### 2. Social Media Content (Instagram / TikTok Stills)

Template:
```
[Platform-optimized: Instagram square 1:1 / Instagram story 9:16 / Facebook 4:5] lifestyle
content photo. [Subject: person with product / product in lifestyle setting / aesthetic flat
lay / candid moment]. [Visual hook: interesting composition, striking color, unusual angle
or perspective]. [Trendy aesthetic: clean girl / dark academia / coastal grandmother /
cottagecore / minimalist / maximalist — match brand]. [Natural or naturally-styled lighting].
Shot on [camera suggesting authentic feel: Fujifilm for film-like / iPhone-style for UGC
aesthetic]. [Color grade: slightly film-like with lifted shadows]. [Composition optimized for
social: subject in center-third, no important elements at edges where UI overlaps]. Social
media content photography, scroll-stopping and shareable. No over-produced studio feel
unless brand demands it, no watermarks.
```

### 3. Campaign Series (Multiple Connected Shots)

When creating a multi-image campaign, generate prompts with a shared visual language:

Step 1 — Lock the campaign DNA:
```
CAMPAIGN DNA:
- Brand: [name/archetype]
- Season/Collection: [name]
- Color world: [3-4 specific colors]
- Lighting signature: [specific consistent setup]
- Film stock: [one stock for whole campaign]
- Environment family: [connected locations]
- Model(s): [use character-consistency skill]
- Mood words: [3 specific mood descriptors]
- Typography space: [consistent placement]
```

Step 2 — Generate each shot referencing the DNA:
```
[Campaign DNA context]
Shot [X] of [total]: [specific scene description]. [Unique pose/action for this shot].
[Same lighting signature]. [Same film stock]. [Same color world]. Maintain visual consistency
with all other shots in this campaign series.
```

### 4. Travel / Destination Lifestyle

Template:
```
Travel lifestyle photograph of [person/couple/group] [authentic travel moment: exploring a
market, sitting at outdoor café, walking along coastline, looking at architecture] in
[specific destination or destination-inspired setting with cultural details]. [Outfit
appropriate to location and activity]. [Natural travel lighting: golden hour, dappled shade,
bright tropical sun]. Shot on [35mm lens for travel documentary feel], [f/2-2.8]. [Kodak
Portra 400 or Ektar 100 depending on vivid/soft preference]. [Wide enough to show location
context]. Travel brand photography, wanderlust-inducing yet authentic. No tourist-trap cliché
poses, no over-produced studio feel, no culturally insensitive styling.
```

### 5. Wellness / Mindful Living

Template:
```
Serene wellness lifestyle photograph of [person in moment of self-care / mindfulness / yoga /
meditation / healthy meal prep / morning routine]. [Minimal, natural styling: comfortable
clothing, no heavy makeup, natural hair]. In [calming environment: sunlit studio / minimalist
home / garden / beach at dawn — specific design details]. [Soft, peaceful lighting: early
morning / diffused overcast / warm candlelight]. Shot on [camera] with [50mm f/1.8]. [Fujifilm
Pro 400H or Portra 160 for soft, airy feel]. [Gentle warm tones, slightly desaturated, lifted
shadows]. Medium shot showing both person and peaceful environment. Wellness brand photography,
calm and aspirational. No intense colors, no dramatic shadows, no energetic poses.
```

## Social Media Platform Specs

- **Instagram Feed**: 1:1 (1080x1080) or 4:5 (1080x1350) — keep subject in center-third
- **Instagram Story/Reels**: 9:16 (1080x1920) — leave top 15% and bottom 20% clear of key elements
- **Facebook Ad**: 4:5 or 1:1 — text overlay area needed
- **TikTok Cover**: 9:16 — strong visual in center
- **Pinterest**: 2:3 (1000x1500) — vertical, detailed, text-friendly
- **Website Hero Banner**: 16:9 or 21:9 — wide, subject on one side, text space on other
- **Email Header**: 3:1 or 2:1 — horizontal, clean, fast-loading visual
